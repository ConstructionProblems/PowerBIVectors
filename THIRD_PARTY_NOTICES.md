# Third-Party Notices

## Natural Earth

Ontario boundaries: Natural Earth 1:10m Admin 1 States/Provinces.
Great Lakes: Natural Earth 1:10m Lakes (Superior, Huron, Erie, Ontario).
Cities/towns: Natural Earth 1:10m Populated Places, extracted for Ontario.

Natural Earth data are public domain. Source downloads were extracted from
the `nvkelso/natural-earth-vector` maintainer mirror during development; the
renderer uses only bundled local JSON/GeoJSON.

- https://www.naturalearthdata.com/downloads/10m-cultural-vectors/10m-admin-1-states-provinces/
- https://www.naturalearthdata.com/downloads/10m-physical-vectors/10m-lakes/
- https://www.naturalearthdata.com/downloads/10m-cultural-vectors/10m-populated-places/
- https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/
